## Combat and system sounds

- Human weapons, base impacts: [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-sounds) by Kenney.
- Tank cannons: [25 CC0 bang / firework SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx) by rubberduck.
- Crawler, spitter and brute attacks: [80 CC0 creature SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx) by rubberduck.
- Brute entrance roar: [CC0 Deep Monster Roar](https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-deep-monster-roar) by trazzz123.
- Wave warning: [Alarm](https://opengameart.org/content/alarm-2) by Frenchyboy.
- Defeat: [Game Over](https://opengameart.org/content/game-over-10) by GreyFrogGames.

All items above are published under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. The in-game victory cue is original and synthesized at runtime from the soundtrack's tonal palette.
